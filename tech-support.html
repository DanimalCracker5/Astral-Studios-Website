How much does it cost?

It depends on the problem, so every job is quoted before we start. Tell us what’s happening and we’ll give you a price. You decide whether to go ahead, and there’s no charge for asking.

Is remote support safe?

Yes. You start the session yourself using a well-known screen-sharing app, you watch everything on your screen, and you can disconnect at any time. We never need your bank or card details to fix your computer.

Where do you do house and office calls?

Around White Lake, Michigan, including Waterford, Highland, Milford, Commerce, Walled Lake, Wixom, and Clarkston. If you’re a little further out, call and ask. Remote help is available anywhere in the US.

What if my computer can’t be fixed?

We’ll tell you straight. If a repair isn’t worth the money, we’ll say so, help you choose a replacement, and move your files and settings over to it.

I think I’ve been scammed. Can you help?

Yes. We check the computer for anything the scammer installed, secure your email and accounts, and walk you through what to do next. If money was involved, call your bank first, then call us.

Do you work with businesses?

Yes. We support small offices and shops on a per-job basis or with a monthly plan. We also build websites and AI automation, so one company can handle your tech end to end.
