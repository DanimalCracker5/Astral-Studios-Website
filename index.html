Astral Projection Game

Ways to play

One world, many ways in

The game is in early access and gets new content often. See what’s new →

Sandbox
The full open world. Earn currency, build your garage, and play it your way.
Your Own Worlds
Create and save your own worlds, and turn on cheats to build without limits.
Racing
Find race tracks scattered around the map, or jump into a Quick Race from your in-game phone.
Arenas & Gunfight
Open the Mini Games app on your phone for wave-based Arenas or fast-paced Gunfight matches.
