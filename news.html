✨ Feature May 2026
Proximity Voice Chat Added to Multiplayer

Multiplayer sessions now support proximity voice chat powered by Dissonance Voice Chat. Players can speak to each other in-game, with audio volume scaling based on how close you are to other players.

✨ Feature May 2026
In-Game Radio Now Playing in Vehicles

A new in-game radio system is now live and will play when you enter a vehicle. Stations features a mix of lo-fi tracks, songs I wrote the lyrics for & songs written by AI. These songs are generated with Suno AI.

⚠️ Notice February 2026
Astral Mobile Edition Temporarily Unavailable on App Store

My Apple Developer license expired in February 2026, which has caused Astral Mobile Edition to be removed from the App Store. Astral is still fully available on Android and Steam, and the VR Edition remains live on the Meta Horizon Store. Plans are in place to restore and expand the iOS release — more details when that's underway.
