Feature October 2026
Mini Games: Quick Races, Gunfights and Arena with a rule editor

New Mini Games app is live.

One app for multiplayer mayhem: Quick Races with 1-10 laps, Gunfights, and Arena matches — all with a rule editor. Bottomless Clip, respawn delay, time limit, grappling hook on/off: you set it, everyone plays it. There’s a lobby browser too, so you can jump into any open match or host your own and let people join. Best part: custom rules work in hosted matches, even offline.

Feature May 2026
Proximity voice chat added to multiplayer

Multiplayer sessions now support proximity voice chat powered by Dissonance Voice Chat. Players can speak to each other in-game, with audio volume scaling based on how close you are to other players.

Feature May 2026
In-game radio now playing in vehicles

A new in-game radio system is now live and will play when you enter a vehicle. Stations feature a mix of lo-fi tracks, songs I wrote the lyrics for, and songs written by AI. These songs are generated with Suno AI.

Notice February 2026
Astral Mobile Edition temporarily unavailable on the App Store

My Apple Developer license expired in February 2026, which caused Astral Mobile Edition to be removed from the App Store. Astral is still fully available on Android and Steam, and the VR Edition remains live on the Meta Horizon Store. Plans are in place to restore and expand the iOS release — more details when that’s underway.

Update — resolved in August 2026. The game is back on the App Store as Astral Projection Game.
