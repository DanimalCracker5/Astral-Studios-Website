Astral Studios LLC SMS consent

Astral Studios LLC may send SMS messages only when a person gives permission to receive text updates.

How SMS consent is collected

During a phone call, a caller may be asked:

“Would you like us to send you a text confirmation or follow-up message about your consultation?”

If the caller says yes, Astral Studios LLC may send SMS messages related to that consultation.

Types of messages
  • Consultation confirmations
  • Follow-up messages about a requested website, software, game development, video editing, or AI automation project
  • Appointment-related updates
Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on the conversation, but is expected to be low.

Opt-out

You can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving text messages.

Help

You can reply HELP for help.

Rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Privacy

Astral Studios LLC does not sell or share SMS consent, phone numbers, or opt-in information with third parties for marketing purposes.
